Artificial Intelligence Capabilities and the Sustainable Resilience of SMEs

Examining how AI capabilities build organizational agility — and how that agility helps small and medium enterprises withstand disruption and stay competitive.

Introduction

Background of Study

Small and medium enterprises are the backbone of the economy, yet they face pressures that shift faster than they can traditionally respond to. This study looks at whether AI capabilities offer a route to lasting resilience.

  1. SMEs operate in increasingly uncertain environments due to technological change, economic uncertainty, changing customer behaviour and competition.
  2. Artificial Intelligence (AI) can help SMEs analyse information, improve decision-making, predict changes and respond more effectively.
  3. However, AI adoption alone is not enough. SMEs need strong AI Infrastructure, AI Skills & Expertise, AI Managerial Alignment and AI Data Governance.
  4. These AI capabilities can strengthen Organizational Agility, allowing SMEs to respond quickly and adapt their operations and resources.
  5. Greater Organizational Agility can ultimately strengthen SME Sustainable Resilience, helping SMEs withstand disruptions and remain competitive over the long term.
AI Capabilities → Organizational Agility → Sustainable Resilience

1.1

Problem Statement

  • SMEs face increasing challenges from technological disruption, economic uncertainty, and competitive pressure.
  • AI can improve decision-making, automation, and business responsiveness, but adoption alone does not guarantee resilience.
  • Existing studies often examine AI capability as a single construct, making it difficult to understand the role of individual AI dimensions.
  • Limited research explains how AI Infrastructure, AI Skills & Expertise, AI Managerial Alignment, and AI Data Governance contribute to SME Sustainable Resilience through Organizational Agility.

Scope of Study

  • Focuses on Malaysian SMEs.
  • Examines four AI capability dimensions: AI Infrastructure, AI Skills & Expertise, AI Managerial Alignment, and AI Data Governance.

Chapter 02

Literature Review

Underpinning theory, variables of study and the conceptual framework.

Underpinning Theory Variables of Study Framework
2.1

Dynamic Capabilities Theory (DCT)

Teece, Pisano & Shuen (1997)

The study uses DCT to explain how SMEs use, adapt and reconfigure their capabilities to respond effectively to changing business environments.

Three Key Processes
01

Sensing

The ability to identify changes, opportunities and threats in the business environment.

AI capabilities can help SMEs obtain and analyse information to detect changes more quickly.
02

Seizing

The ability to make decisions and allocate resources to respond to identified opportunities or threats.

AI can support SMEs by improving decision-making and helping them respond more effectively.
03

Reconfiguring

The ability to adjust organizational resources, processes and capabilities when conditions change.

This allows SMEs to adapt their operations and remain competitive during disruptions.
Application to This Study

AI Infrastructure, AI Skills & Expertise, AI Managerial Alignment and AI Data Governance provide capabilities that can improve an SME’s ability to obtain information and make informed decisions.

These capabilities strengthen Organizational Agility, helping SMEs respond rapidly to environmental changes and ultimately improve SME Sustainable Resilience.

2.2

Variables of Study

This study consists of four independent variables, one mediating variable, and one dependent variable.

Independent Variables (IV)
IV1

AI Infrastructure

The technological resources needed to support AI, including hardware, software, digital systems and platforms.

IV2

AI Skills & Expertise

The knowledge, skills and technical expertise employees need to effectively use and apply AI.

IV3

AI Managerial Alignment

The extent to which management supports AI and aligns AI initiatives with the company’s business objectives.

IV4

AI Data Governance

The policies and procedures used to ensure AI-related data is accurate, secure, reliable and responsibly managed.

Mediating Variable (MV)
MV

Organizational Agility

The ability of an SME to quickly recognize changes and adjust its decisions, processes and resources accordingly.

Dependent Variable (DV)
DV

SME Sustainable Resilience

The ability of an SME to withstand disruptions, adapt to changes, maintain operations and remain viable over the long term.
